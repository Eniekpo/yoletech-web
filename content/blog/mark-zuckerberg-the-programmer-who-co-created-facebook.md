## Who Developed Facebook?

Mark Elliot Zuckerberg is an American computer programmer, entrepreneur, and co-founder of Facebook.

He studied computer science and psychology at Harvard University. His interest in programming and online communication contributed to the development of Facebook.

Although Zuckerberg was the central creator and initial developer, Facebook was launched with other important contributors, including Dustin Moskovitz, Eduardo Saverin, and Chris Hughes.

### When and Where Was Facebook Developed?

Facebook was developed in January 2004 and officially launched on February 4, 2004, at Harvard University in Cambridge, Massachusetts, United States.

The original platform was called  TheFacebook.com

It initially served Harvard students who wanted an online directory where they could create profiles, upload photographs, and connect with other students.

### How Was Facebook Developed?

Zuckerberg developed the initial website using his programming skills while studying at Harvard.

The platform was designed around individual student profiles and social connections.

Its early features included:

* Personal profiles.
* Profile photographs.
* Student information.
* Friend connections.
* Online social networking.

Dustin Moskovitz helped with the technical development and expansion, Chris Hughes contributed to early communication and promotion, and Eduardo Saverin supported the business side and initial financing.

As registration expanded to other universities, Facebook moved its operations to Palo Alto, California, in June 2004.

In September 2006, Facebook opened registration to the general public, allowing people outside universities to join.

### What Problem Did Facebook Solve?

Before Facebook, connecting with classmates and maintaining social relationships online was less centralized.

Universities often had physical student directories, but these did not provide the interactive experience of an online social network.

Facebook introduced a convenient platform where people could create digital identities, find friends, share information, and maintain relationships.

It gradually developed into a platform for communication, entertainment, business promotion, and online communities.

### Why Is Facebook Relevant Today?

Facebook significantly influenced how individuals, businesses, and organizations communicate online.

Its impact includes:

* Social networking: Connecting people across geographical boundaries.
* Digital marketing: Helping businesses reach customers through targeted advertising.
* Community building: Providing groups and pages for shared interests.
* E-commerce: Supporting business promotion and product discovery.
* Digital communication: Changing how people share photographs, videos, news, and information.

In May 2012, Facebook became a publicly traded company through its initial public offering (IPO).

In October 2021, its parent company changed its name from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms, Inc., reflecting its broader focus on social technology and virtual experiences.

Mark Zuckerberg remains one of the most influential figures in the technology industry.

Facebook's development demonstrates how a relatively small university project can grow into a global technology business.
