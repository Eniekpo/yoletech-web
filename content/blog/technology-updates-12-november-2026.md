Six technology developments developers, data analysts and business professionals should know about.

From Google's interactive AI interfaces to AWS local cloud testing, here is what is changing.

**GOOGLE A2UI 1.0**

[Google](https://developers.googleblog.com/a2ui-10-build-delightful-fast-reliable-agentic-experiences/) has released A2UI 1.0, an open protocol that allows AI agents to generate interactive interfaces instead of text-only responses.

Developers can build with React, Angular, Flutter, Python and TypeScript.

**AWS EMULATOR**

[AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/test-your-applications-against-aws-locally-with-aws-emulator/) has launched a local emulator for testing supported AWS services.

Developers can test cloud application logic using existing SDKs and CLI commands without deploying to AWS.

Useful for backend development and AI coding agents.

**DATABRICKS GENIE**

[Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/databricks-launches-new-always-genie-autonomous-ai-complex) has announced an always-on autonomous AI agent for enterprise work.

It can monitor business information, delegate tasks and notify employees when decisions are required.

A significant development for business intelligence and analytics.

**GOOGLE SEO GUIDANCE**

[Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/11/crawling-cdn) has explained why CDN migrations can temporarily increase origin-server traffic.

Its recommendations include warming up caches and allowing verified search crawlers.

Important for WordPress developers and SEO professionals.

**GITHUB AND STRIPE**

[GitHub](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-11-issue-types-and-fields-are-available-for-personal-accounts/) has introduced custom issue types and fields for personal accounts.

[Stripe](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripehsbc-power-tap-to-pay-for-uk-small-businesses) and HSBC have also introduced smartphone-based Tap to Pay for eligible UK small businesses.

Developer productivity and payment infrastructure continue to improve.

**THE BIGGER PICTURE**

AI is moving towards task execution, interactive applications and automated business workflows.

Developers should pay attention to these changes and learn how to apply them responsibly.
