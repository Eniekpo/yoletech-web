### Technology updates worth following today.

Google advances AI-powered coding, PostgreSQL addresses critical security issues, OpenAI introduces business profiles, and GitHub improves secret detection.

Here are four developments and what they mean for professionals.

**GOOGLE: GEMINI 4 HYDROGEN**

Google has introduced [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/), a model designed for faster coding, debugging and AI-agent workflows.

Developers can access it through Google AI Studio, the Gemini API and supported Google products.

Google is also bringing the model into Search to improve responses to complex, multi-part questions.

**POSTGRESQL: 52 SECURITY FIXES**

The [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/postgresql-187-1712-1616-1520-1425-and-19-rc-2-released-3442/) Global Development Group has released updates for supported database versions, fixing 52 security vulnerabilities and more than 140 bugs.

Important: PostgreSQL 14 has reached end-of-life and will no longer receive security updates.

Database administrators should review their versions, apply the appropriate patches and plan upgrades.

**OPENAI: BUSINESSES IN CHATGPT**

OpenAI has introduced Businesses in [ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) in beta.

Eligible businesses can claim their profiles, update business information and access aggregated engagement insights.

Business Agents will also allow eligible businesses to interact with customers directly through ChatGPT.

Business Agent availability initially targets eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses.

**GITHUB: BETTER SECRET DETECTION**

GitHub has added [Cloudflare Access service-token detection](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-12-secret-scanning-adds-a-detector-for-cloudflare/) to its secret-scanning feature.

This helps identify exposed credentials in repositories and alerts developers when supported secrets are discovered.

For developers, this is another reason to avoid storing API keys and access tokens directly in source code.

**THE BIGGER PICTURE**

AI is changing how software gets built and how businesses get discovered. At the same time, database maintenance and credential security remain essential.

Keep learning. Keep building. Keep up with technology.
