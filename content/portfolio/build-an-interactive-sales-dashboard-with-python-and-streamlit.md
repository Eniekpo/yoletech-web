## Executive Overview

Built a responsive, cloud-deployed **Sales Analytics Suite** using Python, Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit to replace static spreadsheet packs with real-time self-service Business Intelligence.

### Key Architectural Highlights
- **Dynamic Multi-Dimensional Filtering:** Instant slicing by region, product category, sales channel, and date range with zero page-reload latency.
- **Automated Data Hygiene Pipeline:** Vectorized Pandas preprocessing that normalizes dates, resolves missing values, and computes margin KPIs automatically on upload.
- **Interactive Plotly Visuals:** Time-series revenue trends, category Pareto charts, and regional performance heatmaps.
