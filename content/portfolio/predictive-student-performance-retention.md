### Project Scope & Objective
Developed an early-warning predictive classification model to identify students at risk of non-completion 4 weeks before critical evaluation milestones.

### Implementation & Results
- Applied logistic regression and random forest classification on multi-dimensional engagement data.
- Achieved **92.4% predictive accuracy** with cross-validation.
- Connected model outputs to interactive Tableau executive dashboards enabling advisors to proactively engage struggling learners.
