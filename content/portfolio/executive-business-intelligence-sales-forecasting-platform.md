## Executive Overview

Designed and deployed a unified **Executive Business Intelligence & Sales Forecasting Platform** that consolidates multi-branch transactional data into a single governed source of truth.

### Key Architectural Highlights
- **Star-Schema Data Modeling:** Structured fact and dimension tables optimized for sub-second drill-downs across territories, SKUs, and customer segments.
- **Rolling Revenue Forecasting:** Integrated Python time-series forecasting models to project 30/60/90-day demand and highlight inventory risk early.
- **Executive KPI Hierarchy:** Variance-to-target scorecards, gross margin waterfalls, and automated anomaly alerts.
