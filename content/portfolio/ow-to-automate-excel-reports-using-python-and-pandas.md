## Executive Overview

Developed a reproducible **Python & Pandas Excel Automation Pipeline** that replaces hours of repetitive weekly spreadsheet consolidation with a single deterministic script execution.

### Key Architectural Highlights
- **Multi-File Ingestion & Validation:** Automatically scans raw departmental exports, standardizes column headers, and flags schema or data-type mismatches.
- **Programmatic Workbook Styling:** Uses Pandas and OpenPyXL to generate pivot tables, conditional formatting, currency formatting, and embedded KPI summary tabs.
- **Zero-Touch Scheduling:** Designed for unattended execution with audit logging and automated email/webhook delivery.
