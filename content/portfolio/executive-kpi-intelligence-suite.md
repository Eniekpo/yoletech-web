### Executive Summary & Problem Context
Prior to this initiative, departmental metrics were compiled manually across disconnected spreadsheets, requiring up to 3 business days of preparation for board reviews with frequent cross-departmental reconciliation errors.

### The Architectural Blueprint
- **ETL Ingestion:** Automated Python ingestion scripts extracting data from relational SQL databases and operational logs.
- **Data Modeling:** Star-schema dimensional modeling enforcing single-source-of-truth business logic across revenue, utilization, and project milestones.
- **Visualization:** Interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards featuring custom drill-down hierarchies, dynamic time-intelligence filtering, and automated refresh schedules.

### Measurable Business Impact
- **65% reduction** in executive report generation time.
- **99.8% compliance** in data validation and discrepancy auditing.
- Empowered leadership with real-time mobile and desktop decision clarity.
