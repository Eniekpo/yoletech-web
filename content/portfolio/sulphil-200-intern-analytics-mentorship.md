### Program Overview
Led structured training bootcamps and hands-on laboratory sessions for over 200 data analytics interns and corporate team members.

### Core Curricular Modules
- **Python for Data Science:** Pandas, NumPy, statistical auditing, and data wrangling.
- **Relational Databases & SQL:** Schema normalization, window functions, and indexing strategies.
- **Executive BI Storytelling:** Power BI and Tableau dashboard principles, color theory, and stakeholder communication.
