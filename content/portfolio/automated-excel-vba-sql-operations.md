### Problem Context
The operations and inventory department spent over 18 labor hours every week manually copying, reconciling, and auditing 6 departmental Excel workbooks, creating frequent formula corruption and reporting delays.

### The Technical Solution
- Developed hardened Excel VBA macros with defensive cell validation and error-trapping.
- Built automated Python scripts to parse, clean, and sync validated rows into SQL Server staging tables.
- Implemented automated email status reporting and discrepancy alerts.

### Business Impact
- **18+ weekly hours saved**, freeing operational analysts for strategic capacity planning.
- **100% elimination** of formula corruption and duplicate entry errors.
